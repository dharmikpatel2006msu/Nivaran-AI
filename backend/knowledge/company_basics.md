## About ShopNest

<!-- category: company_basics | subtopic: company_overview | escalate: false | keywords: about shopnest, who are you, what is shopnest, company overview, what do you sell -->

ShopNest is an online retail marketplace offering products across categories including electronics, fashion, home & kitchen, beauty, groceries, and books. The platform operates as a hybrid marketplace: some items are sold and shipped directly by ShopNest, while others are sold by **verified third-party sellers** and fulfilled either by the seller or through ShopNest's fulfillment network — the product page always indicates which of the two applies, since this affects return handling and delivery timelines.

ShopNest was founded with the goal of making everyday shopping simple, affordable, and reliable, particularly for customers in tier-2 and tier-3 cities who historically had limited access to a wide product catalog. The company is headquartered in Bengaluru, India, with regional fulfillment centers located across multiple states to support faster delivery.

ShopNest is a purely online platform — there are currently no physical retail stores where customers can walk in, browse, or return items in person. All purchases, support, and returns are handled through the app, website, or designated courier pickup.

**Sample customer question:** "What kind of company is ShopNest, and do you sell things yourselves or is it like a marketplace?"
**Sample answer:** "ShopNest is an online marketplace — some products are sold and shipped directly by us, and others by verified third-party sellers on our platform. You can always check the product page to see which one applies, since it affects things like returns and delivery time. We don't have physical stores; everything is handled through the app or website."

## Business Hours & Customer Support Availability

<!-- category: company_basics | subtopic: support_hours | escalate: false | keywords: business hours, support hours, when are you open, customer service timing, holiday support -->

ShopNest's online store is available for browsing and placing orders **24/7**, including weekends and public holidays — there is no "closing time" for shopping itself. Customer support availability, however, varies by channel:

- **In-app live chat and chatbot support** (this assistant) are available 24/7.
- **Phone support** is available daily from **9:00 AM to 9:00 PM IST**, including weekends.
- **Email support** is monitored 24/7, but responses are generally sent within **24–48 hours**, longer during high-volume periods like sale events or major holidays.

During major national holidays (e.g., Diwali, New Year's Day), phone support hours may be reduced or the queue may run longer than usual, though this is announced in advance via a banner in the app. Order processing and dispatch may also be delayed by 1–2 business days around these dates due to courier partner holiday schedules, separate from support availability itself.

**Sample customer question:** "Is customer support available right now, or only during certain hours?"
**Sample answer:** "Chat support like this is available anytime, 24/7. Phone support runs from 9 AM to 9 PM IST daily, and email is monitored around the clock but typically takes 24–48 hours for a reply. During major holidays, phone wait times can be a bit longer, but that's usually flagged in the app ahead of time."

## Shipping Coverage & Delivery Areas

<!-- category: company_basics | subtopic: shipping_coverage | escalate: false | keywords: do you ship to, delivery areas, international shipping, pincode not serviceable, remote area delivery -->

ShopNest currently ships **domestically within India only**; international shipping is not supported at this time, and orders cannot be placed with a delivery address outside the country. Coverage spans the vast majority of serviceable pincodes across urban and semi-urban areas, but a small number of remote or restricted pincodes (parts of certain hill states, some island territories, and a few conflict-sensitive regions) are **not currently serviceable** — this is checked automatically at checkout using the delivery pincode, and unserviceable addresses will be flagged before an order can be placed.

For serviceable remote or rural pincodes, delivery timelines are typically longer than for metro areas (often 5–9 business days versus 2–4 for metros), and a **remote area delivery surcharge** may apply on certain heavy or bulky items — this is shown transparently in the order summary before checkout, never added afterward.

Customers relocating or shipping gifts to a different city can change the delivery address for an order **only before it has been dispatched**; once a shipment leaves the fulfillment center, the delivery address is locked and cannot be modified, as this would violate courier handling protocols.

**Sample customer question:** "Can you deliver to my hometown, and do you ship outside India?"
**Sample answer:** "We only ship within India at the moment, no international delivery yet. As for your hometown, you can check by entering the pincode at checkout — the vast majority of areas are covered, though a few remote pincodes aren't serviceable yet, and delivery to rural areas can take a bit longer than metro cities."

## Terms of Service, Privacy Policy & Data Use

<!-- category: company_basics | subtopic: policies_overview | escalate: false | keywords: terms of service, privacy policy, how is my data used, terms and conditions, data sharing -->

ShopNest's full **Terms of Service** and **Privacy Policy** are available under **Account > Legal** in the app and on the website footer. At a high level: customer data (name, contact details, delivery address, order history) is used to process orders, provide support, and personalize product recommendations; it is **not sold to third parties**, though limited data (e.g., name, address, contact number) is necessarily shared with courier partners solely to complete delivery, and with payment gateways solely to process transactions.

This assistant can explain policies in general terms but **cannot provide legal advice or interpret contractual obligations** in a binding way — for questions requiring a definitive legal interpretation (e.g., a dispute over contract terms, a data protection authority inquiry, or a formal legal notice), the query should be routed to the legal/compliance team rather than answered directly, per the Corporate & Legal Information section below.

Customers can request a copy of their personal data or request account-linked data deletion (separate from full account deletion) under **Account > Privacy > Data Requests**, consistent with applicable data protection regulations; such requests are processed within the timeframe stated in the Privacy Policy, typically **30 days**.

**Sample customer question:** "Does ShopNest sell my personal information to other companies?"
**Sample answer:** "No, we don't sell customer data to third parties. We do share the minimum necessary information — like your delivery address — with couriers and payment gateways so they can complete your order, but that's strictly for fulfilling the transaction. You can read the full Privacy Policy under Account > Legal, and you're also able to request a copy or deletion of your data from there."

## Careers at ShopNest

<!-- category: company_basics | subtopic: careers | escalate: false | keywords: jobs, careers, hiring, work at shopnest, job openings, internship -->

ShopNest hires across corporate functions (engineering, product, marketing, operations, finance) as well as warehouse and logistics roles at its fulfillment centers. Open positions are posted on the **Careers** page of the ShopNest website, and applications are submitted directly there — this assistant and phone/chat support **do not process job applications, forward resumes, or schedule interviews**, since hiring is handled by a separate recruitment team and system.

Customers or applicants asking about internship programs, campus hiring, or walk-in warehouse hiring drives should be directed to the Careers page, which lists current openings, eligibility criteria, and application deadlines where applicable; this information changes frequently enough that the assistant should not guess at specific current openings from memory.

Unsolicited resume submissions via support email or chat are not reviewed by the recruitment team and may not receive a response, so applicants should always use the official Careers page rather than general support channels.

**Sample customer question:** "How do I apply for a job at ShopNest?"
**Sample answer:** "You can find all current openings and apply directly through the Careers page on our website — that's handled by a separate recruitment team, so I'm not able to take resumes or schedule interviews through chat or phone support, but the Careers page will have everything you need including eligibility details and how to apply."

## Becoming a Seller / Business Partnerships

<!-- category: company_basics | subtopic: seller_partnerships | escalate: false | keywords: become a seller, sell on shopnest, vendor registration, business partnership, become a supplier -->

Businesses interested in selling products on ShopNest can register through the **"Sell on ShopNest"** portal, accessible from the website footer, which requires business registration details, GST/tax identification (where applicable), bank account details for payouts, and product catalog information. Approval is not automatic — submitted applications are reviewed by the seller onboarding team, and processing typically takes **3–7 business days**, though this can vary based on category and documentation completeness.

Once approved, sellers get access to a separate **Seller Dashboard** for managing listings, inventory, orders, and payouts, which is entirely distinct from the customer-facing app and support system described elsewhere in this document. Seller-specific support (listing issues, payout queries, catalog disputes) is handled by a dedicated **Seller Support** team, not general customer support — a customer asking a seller-side question should be redirected there rather than answered as if it were a customer order query.

ShopNest also periodically explores brand partnerships, co-marketing collaborations, and bulk/corporate purchasing arrangements; inquiries of this nature should be directed to the **Business Partnerships** contact listed on the website's "Partner With Us" page rather than routed through standard customer support.

**Sample customer question:** "I run a small business — how do I start selling my products on ShopNest?"
**Sample answer:** "You can register through the 'Sell on ShopNest' portal linked in our website footer — you'll need your business registration and tax details, and bank details for payouts. Applications are reviewed by our onboarding team and usually take about 3–7 business days. Once approved, you'll manage everything through a separate Seller Dashboard, and any seller-specific questions after that go through our dedicated Seller Support team rather than general customer support."

## Corporate & Legal Information / Regulatory Complaints

<!-- category: company_basics | subtopic: corporate_legal | escalate: true | keywords: legal notice, grievance officer, regulatory complaint, consumer forum, company registration, legal action, cease and desist -->

ShopNest's registered corporate entity, registered office address, CIN (Corporate Identification Number), and GST registration details are published under **Account > Legal > Corporate Information** in the app and in the footer of the website, in compliance with applicable consumer protection and e-commerce regulations. As with other legal/regulatory content, this assistant can point customers to where this information is published but should not attempt to recite specific registration numbers from memory, since these are subject to change and must be sourced from the authoritative in-app/website page.

Any communication indicating a **formal legal notice, a consumer forum/court complaint already filed, a regulatory or data-protection authority inquiry, correspondence from a customer's lawyer, or an explicit threat of legal action** against ShopNest should **always be escalated immediately to a human agent** (routed to the Legal/Compliance team) rather than handled through standard support troubleshooting — the bot should not attempt to resolve, admit fault on behalf of the company, or negotiate any outcome in these cases.

The **Grievance Officer** contact required under applicable e-commerce regulation is published under Account > Legal > Corporate Information; complaints specifically addressed to the Grievance Officer, or customers stating they intend to escalate to a consumer forum, should be treated with the same urgency as above.

**Escalation threshold:** This is escalate-eligible on the first report with no repeat-message requirement — legal/regulatory matters carry compounding risk (statutory response deadlines, documentation requirements) if not routed to the right human team promptly.

**Sample customer question:** "I'm filing a complaint with the consumer forum against ShopNest if this isn't resolved — I also want your company's registration details for the complaint."
**Sample answer:** "I understand this is frustrating, and I want to make sure it's handled properly. I've raised this directly with our Legal/Compliance team as a priority so a specialist can follow up with you. Our registered company details, including our Grievance Officer's contact information, are published under Account > Legal > Corporate Information in the app — I'd recommend referencing that directly for your complaint."

## Sustainability & Corporate Social Responsibility

<!-- category: company_basics | subtopic: sustainability_csr | escalate: false | keywords: sustainability, eco friendly packaging, csr, plastic packaging, social responsibility, carbon footprint -->

ShopNest has been progressively shifting toward **recyclable and reduced-plastic packaging** across its fulfillment centers, including paper-based void fill and recyclable mailer bags for eligible product categories; the rollout is ongoing and not yet uniform across every category or region, so some shipments may still use standard packaging depending on the product and originating warehouse.

The company runs periodic **CSR initiatives**, including partnerships with local NGOs for donation drives (customers can occasionally opt to round up their order total to a charitable cause at checkout during specific campaigns) and support for skilling programs aimed at warehouse and logistics workforce development in the regions where ShopNest operates fulfillment centers.

Customers wishing to recycle packaging or return-eligible items for refurbishment/donation programs (where offered) can find category-specific instructions on the product or order page during active campaigns; general packaging recycling (breaking down cardboard, removing plastic mailers per local municipal guidelines) is otherwise the customer's responsibility, as ShopNest does not currently offer reverse packaging pickup outside of standard product returns.

**Sample customer question:** "Does ShopNest do anything for sustainability, like eco-friendly packaging?"
**Sample answer:** "Yes — we've been rolling out recyclable and reduced-plastic packaging like paper void-fill and recyclable mailers, though it's still being expanded across all categories and warehouses, so you might occasionally see standard packaging depending on the item. We also run periodic CSR initiatives, including donation drives and workforce skilling programs in the regions where we operate."
